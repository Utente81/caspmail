## Slide 2: The Problem
**Standard Communication is the Weakest Link**
- In an era of relentless cyber warfare, traditional email and communication platforms are highly vulnerable.
- Passwords are easily stolen via phishing and credential harvesting.
- Data breaches cost millions in fines (GDPR, PCI-DSS) and reputational damage.
- Existing solutions lack real-time visibility and autonomous incident response.

## Slide 3: The Solution
**Military-Grade Communication Fused with a SOC**
- CasperMail is not just an encrypted email client.
- It is a comprehensive platform designed for organizations where data confidentiality is a strict legal and operational requirement.
- **Zero-Trust Architecture**: Powered by HashiCorp Vault, even if the application layer is compromised, attackers cannot establish persistence.

---

## Slide 4: Key Features & Unique Selling Propositions (USPs)
**Why CasperMail?**
1. **Passwordless by Design**: Native WebAuthn (Passkeys) via hardware biometrics (Touch ID, YubiKeys). Phishing becomes obsolete.
2. **Client-Side DLP**: Data Loss Prevention directly in the browser. Blocks PII (Credit Cards, IBANs) before it reaches the network.
3. **Real-Time SOC & 3D Threat Map**: WebGL-powered 3D globe visualizing cyber attacks in real-time.
4. **Enterprise SIEM Export**: Seamless integration with existing SOC workflows (Splunk, IBM QRadar) via Webhooks.
